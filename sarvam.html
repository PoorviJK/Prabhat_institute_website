About Sarvam Kuteeram

Located in Yelahanka, Bangalore, Sarvam Kuteeram is a Gurukul Learning Space. We emphasize an ancient Indian educational tradition, holistic learning, and character development. We strive to preserve and adapt this traditional system to empower young minds in the modern world.

We provide a nurturing environment where students not only acquire academic knowledge but also learn important life skills, ethics, and values. We deeply believe in the philosophy of "Guru-Shishya Parampara", integrating Vedic learning with the future of AI and ML.

  • Holistic Curriculum: Integrating Vedic studies, yoga, meditation, traditional arts, sciences, math, and robotics.
  • Homeschooling: Tailored educational programs with academic support and subscription-based extracurricular activities.
  • Workshops: Emotional Intelligence, storytelling, Electronics, Art, and Spoken Kannada.
  • Vocational & After-School: Tailoring, Theatre, Carpentry, Vedic Maths, Robotics, Abacus, Memory Techniques, and Olympiad preparation.
